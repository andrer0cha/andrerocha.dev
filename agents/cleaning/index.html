The first AI agent for cleaning business software

Residential cleaning runs on texts: reschedules, add-ons, "is she coming today?". Most of them arrive when the office is closed.

What it handles alone

  • Moving a clean to another open slot with the same cleaner or team
  • Confirming tomorrow's visit and arrival window
  • Answering what's included in a standard or deep clean, from your client's own service list
  • Collecting the details of a new quote request: home size, frequency, pets, date

What it always hands to staff

  • Prices for add-ons or anything not on the service list
  • Complaints, damage, refunds and re-cleans
  • Cancellations inside the late-cancel window
  • Anything about a specific cleaner as a person

Why it matters

A recurring client can be worth thousands of dollars a year to a cleaning company. A reschedule left unanswered overnight becomes a skipped visit, and sometimes a lost client. The agent answers in minutes, in the business's own voice, and leaves every judgment call to the people who make it today.
