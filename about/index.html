About

I'm André, a software engineer with ten years of experience. For the last year I've built and run customer-facing AI agents in production.

Today I help run customer-facing AI agents at a US vertical SaaS, in production, talking to real customers every day. I've watched an agent grow from one narrow job into a much bigger one, and learned what that takes. Most of my work is the unglamorous part: deciding what it should never do, finding out when it was wrong, and fixing it before a customer notices.

I live in Madrid and work in English, Spanish and Portuguese.
