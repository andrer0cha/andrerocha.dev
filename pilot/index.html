First Agent Pilot

The first AI agent inside your product, for one job, live with real customers in about five weeks.

  1. Understand

    I talk to the people who answer your customers today and read real conversations. We pick one job, and write down what the agent does alone and what it always hands to a person.

  2. Build

    The agent lives inside your product, connected to your calendar, records and messaging. Your team keeps full control of what it can do.

  3. Connect

    Handoffs to staff, a daily summary for the team, and a simple way to flag a bad answer and fix it.

  4. Test

    Hundreds of realistic conversations, including the hard ones (urgent, angry, ambiguous, after hours), before any customer sees it.

  5. Launch and measure

    Live with a few accounts. I read every conversation the first week and report what it handled alone, what it handed off and what it got wrong.

  • A test set of realistic conversations you can re-run after every change

Good first jobs

  • Reschedule and cancellation requests that arrive after hours
  • Missed-call follow-up by text
  • "Is my tech on the way?" and appointment reminders that people answer
  • Payment and invoice questions
  • New inquiries that need two or three questions before staff can quote

Not a good first job: anything where a wrong answer is dangerous or irreversible. The agent hands those to a person, always.
