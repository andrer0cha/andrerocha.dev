Data deletion

You can ask for every message and detail an agent I run holds about you to be deleted.

How to ask

  1. Email me@andrerocha.dev with the subject "Data deletion request".
  2. Include the phone number you used to write to the business, and the business's name.
  3. I confirm by email within 15 days, once your conversations, summaries and attachments have been deleted from the agent and its backups.

What is deleted

Your messages, voice-note transcripts, images, the summary prepared for the business's staff, and your phone number. The business you were talking to may keep its own copy of the conversation in its WhatsApp app; to remove it there, ask the business directly.

If you connected a business account

If you are a business that connected your WhatsApp Business account to a service I run and want it removed, email the same address. I disconnect the account and delete its data within 30 days.
