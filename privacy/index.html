What this policy covers

It covers two things: this website, and the customer-facing AI agents I build and operate for businesses (for example, an assistant that answers a studio's WhatsApp messages). For those agents, the business that uses them decides why and how its customers' data is used. I process that data on the business's behalf and only to run the agent.

Data the agents handle

  • Phone number and profile name of the person writing to the business.
  • The content of the messages, including text, images and voice notes sent in the conversation.
  • Details the person shares about what they want (for example, a tattoo idea, placement and size), and a short summary prepared for the business's staff.
  • Technical records needed to deliver messages and fix problems, such as message IDs and timestamps.

Why it is used

Only to reply to the person on the business's behalf, pass the conversation to the business's staff with a summary, and improve the quality of the agent's replies for that same business. Data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared between businesses.

Who else processes it

  • Meta (WhatsApp Business Platform), to send and receive messages.
  • The AI model provider used to write replies and transcribe voice notes (currently OpenAI), under terms that do not allow it to train on this data.
  • The hosting provider where the agent runs.

How long it is kept

Conversations are kept while the business uses the service, and deleted within 30 days after the business stops using it or asks for deletion. A person can ask for their own data to be deleted at any time (see Data deletion).

Your rights

Under the Brazilian LGPD and, where it applies, the EU GDPR, you can ask to access, correct or delete your data, or object to how it is used. Write to me@andrerocha.dev, or to the business you were talking to, and I will reply within 15 days.

This website

This site has no ads and no cookies. It uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits per page, referring site, country and device type; it does not use cookies, fingerprinting or any data that identifies you. If you email me, I keep the email only to reply to you.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
